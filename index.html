ABOUT FURI

Built for automotive.
Connected globally.

Furi Supply Chain Technology Co., Ltd. was established in September 2020. Its predecessor, Taizhou Furi Logistics Co., Ltd., began operations in February 2017. The company focuses on automotive logistics and aims to become an integrated supply-chain service provider with global influence.

  • ✓
    Industry-backed foundationEstablished through the cooperation of Hangzhou Fuju Logistics and Geely Group Investment.
  • ✓
    Integrated operationsConnecting transportation, warehousing, packaging and production support.
  • ✓
    Technology-enabled managementDigital systems support visibility, coordination and operational efficiency.
